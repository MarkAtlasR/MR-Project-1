Biography

Hello my name is Mark Rosales, I have been a Graphic design and media major student since fall of 2021. Art and design is something I have always been passionate about and I strive to improve and better my skills.

I was born and grew up in California for the first 10 years of my life, around 2014 I moved to Las Vegas where I gained an appreciation of concept art and behind the scenes aspect of any creative project. So I desired to become a concept artist which eventually led me to graphic design work.
